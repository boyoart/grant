**original_problem_statement**:
Build a mobile-first web application MVP for a foodstuff business called FoodNova, operating in Nigeria.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS**:
- **Authentication**: Phone + OTP for customers, Email + Password for admins.
- **Customer Features**:
    - Product browsing by categories (name, price, unit, stock).
    - Cart management (add/remove/adjust quantity).
    - Checkout with fulfillment options: Pickup (time slots) or Delivery (zoned pricing).
- **Order Flow**:
    - Order status starts as Pending Payment.
    - Admin confirms payment manually.
    - Stock is deducted *only* upon payment confirmation.
    - Statuses: Pending Payment, Confirmed, Packing, Ready for Pickup/Out for Delivery, Picked Up/Delivered, Completed, Cancelled.
- **Payment**: Manual bank transfer. App shows bank details and Order ID for narration. User can upload proof of payment.
- **Admin Features**:
    - Order management (view, update status, view payment proof).
    - Product & Inventory management (CRUD, stock levels, view stock history).
    - Delivery zone and fee management.
    - General settings (store details, bank info, SMS templates).
- **Notifications**: SMS notifications for key order status changes.
- **Tech Stack**: Mobile-first React frontend, FastAPI backend, MongoDB.
- **User-added features**: Payment proof upload, product search.

**User's preferred language**: English
